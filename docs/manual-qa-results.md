### Current-book target checkpoint event contract

`current_book_target_save_attempted` and `current_book_target_save_completed` / `current_book_target_save_failed` are emitted by `CurrentBookTargetPersistence.persistCurrentBookTarget()` in the Reader operation boundary. Allowed dimensions: `screen=reader`, `action=save_current_book_target`, `operation=current_book_target_save`, `stage`, `outcome`, `entry_point=reading_duration_threshold|reader_close`, `book_type` (enum-derived), `is_retry` (Boolean), and failure-only `reason_code=current_book_target_save_failed`. No UUID/title/cover/path is included. One attempt has exactly one terminal event when persistence returns or throws; a `CancellationException` emits a terminal `cancelled` breadcrumb and is rethrown without a failure event/non-fatal. Correlated start/terminal breadcrumbs use a diagnostic-only correlation ID. The 60-second `CurrentBookTargetCheckpoint` is one-shot per successful Reader session; a successful checkpoint suppresses the close duplicate, while a failed checkpoint leaves a later close eligible for one marked retry. Unexpected persistence exceptions are reported once at this boundary. Local DebugAnalyticsManager observations are not Firebase delivery evidence.
