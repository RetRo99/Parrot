# Case 420 — per-profile Reader Settings isolation (Samsung)

- **Date:** 2026-09-28 (device local time CEST, UTC+0200).
- **Device:** physical Samsung `RFCWC0SSVDM`, SM-S921B, Android 16/API 36, One UI 8.0, gesture navigation.
- **Package/build:** `com.retro99.parrot` 0.4.5 (21), debug; PID `29954` during observation. APK SHA-256 `8ac931ab87b487f88951d39968cdce53c7df193c624b4b6f1f6d4b282279f3d1`; installed APK and local build hashes matched.

- **Privacy:** only disposable profiles A and B are identified here by test role. Actual profile labels/IDs and screenshots are not retained.

## Functional execution

1. In disposable profile A, Reader Settings opened from App Settings. The Theme was changed to Sepia and saved successfully. The observed settings were Sepia and font size 100%.
2. Switched to profile B and opened the same route. Before editing B, the Theme was System and font size was 100%, confirming A's Sepia selection had not leaked.
3. In B, selected Light and increased font size once, from 100% to 105%.
4. Switched to A and reopened Reader Settings: Sepia and 100% remained selected.
5. Switched back to B and reopened Reader Settings: Light and 105% remained selected.

The final screenshots were visually inspected on the phone; they showed the selected Theme control and corresponding preview palette, and the exact displayed font-size values. Screenshots were not saved. No cold process restart was performed. Thus the **Theme and Font Size profile-switch isolation variant passes**; overall case 420 remains **PARTIAL PASS**, not a claim that every Reader setting or restart behavior was exercised.
