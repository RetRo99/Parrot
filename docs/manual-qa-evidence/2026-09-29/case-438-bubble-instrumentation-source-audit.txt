## Reachability and action map

- Bubble is composed only when `currentlyReading`, loaded `bubblePosition`, and `showContinueReading` are present/enabled. It is excluded from Reader, BooksList, BookDetail, Statistics, App Settings, Server Management, Sync & Backup and Reader Settings; it is reachable on Series routes. Cases 425/439/543–545/563/720/740–745/748–749 cover adjacent visibility, stale-target, rotation, overlap, deletion, lifecycle, exit, accessibility and observability variants; new focused extensions are 773–775.
- `DraggableFloatingBubble` starts at a normalized Y fraction, clamps both axes to container bounds, chooses START/END by release center, springs to the edge, and calls `onPositionChanged` on drag end. `detectDragGestures` consumes movement after drag recognition; click is provided by `ContinueReadingBubble`. Drag cancellation changes only transient `isDragging` state and does not invoke the persistence callback. Initial measurement is hidden until position is ready; no explicit visible loading/error state exists if the observer never emits.
- `HomeNavigation` sends `UpdateBubblePosition`; `HomeNavigationViewModel.saveBubblePosition` synchronously serializes/writes the user-scoped preference. No attempt/success/failure/cancel Analytics, breadcrumb, exception boundary, or user-facing retry exists at this write boundary. A thrown preference write can therefore silently lose the new position on restart (not yet runtime-injected).
- Bubble/shelf taps send the same `RequestOpenReader` intent as Book Detail. `handleRequestOpenReader` either shows a playback-conflict dialog or navigates. Conflict dismissal has no source-attributed cancellation signal. Existing `ReaderAnalyticsEvent.BookOpened` and `BookOpenFailed` are emitted at usable publication and initialization/publication failure respectively, but do not carry the Continue Reading source; non-startup reader-open diagnostics have no source route/correlation context. `NavigationAnalyticsEvent.ContinueReadingLaunched(bookUuid)` exists but has no call sites, and its `book_uuid` parameter is excluded by the Analytics allowlist. These are source-confirmed coverage gaps; no incorrect route or runtime exception was observed in this source-only audit.
