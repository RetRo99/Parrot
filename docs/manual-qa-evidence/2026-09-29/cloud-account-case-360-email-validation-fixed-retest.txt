The error cleared after correction; Password remained empty. Tapped Password to commit focus-loss validation. [Screenshot](cloud-account-email-validation-fixed-valid.png).
4. Cleared the synthetic email with Delete, pressed system Back once to dismiss the keyboard (screen remained Cloud Account), and confirmed both fields empty and Sign in disabled. [Clean final form](cloud-account-email-validation-fixed-clean.png).
