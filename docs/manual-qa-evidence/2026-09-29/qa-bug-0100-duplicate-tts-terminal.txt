- **Actual:** One `attempted` event was followed by two byte-identical `succeeded` Analytics events and two duplicate terminal breadcrumbs, all sharing one correlation ID. The Reader briefly exposed the Pause control; Android's media audio accounting showed one short playback segment.
- **Frequency:** Confirmed once during one deliberate successful playback action; no second controlled success attempt yet.

  The permission prompt capture is [here](case-417-play-permission-dialog.png). After the temporary grant, `TtsRouter` confirmed `voice=null engine=SYSTEM`; Samsung media accounting recorded a single approximately 4.6s audio segment. The denied attempt was classified as ordinary `cancelled(reason_code=permission_denied)` without an exception. `DebugAnalyticsManager` is local-only; this is not Firebase Analytics ingestion evidence.
- **Root cause:** Confirmed at the reporting boundary: the Android controller's `finishPlaybackSucceeded` emits one `Succeeded` object for this attempt, while a ReaderViewModel playback-operation collector maps each received object to both one analytics event and one breadcrumb. Runtime therefore had duplicate active collectors or equivalent duplicate delivery. Exact owner/lifecycle source of the extra collector remains under investigation; do not narrow it to a specific lifecycle cause until confirmed.
