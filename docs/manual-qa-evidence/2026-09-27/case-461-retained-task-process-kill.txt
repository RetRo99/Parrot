Case 461 — kill Reader's background process while retaining its Recents task
Date: 2026-09-27, Samsung local time CEST (UTC+0200).

Device/build
- Physical Samsung `RFCWC0SSVDM`, SM-S921B (Galaxy S24), Android 16/API 36, One UI 8.0 build `S921BXXSGDZG1`, gesture navigation.
- `com.retro99.parrot` 0.4.5 (21), target SDK 36, debug, QA-BUG-0026 fix commit `b18cd671f5a54b504a1b39e6aa9e33918f25dfaa`; local/Samsung-pulled APK SHA-256 `2c5e6ec146c89fc8a4ebe8494a46949dd74104a18b38f4bc5556de25abf4b67b`.
- Default local profile, local imported *Pride and Prejudice*, saved position page 29/64 (49%). No credentials or app-data reset.

Preconditions and exact steps
1. Opened the local EPUB in Reader at page 29/64 (49%). A 60-second current-book checkpoint succeeded at 03:13:10.719 CEST (PID `2412`, correlation ID `364ab324-d5d9-4499-93e7-0b1483c4dfbc`). Pre-kill screen: [`case-461-reader-before-background-kill.png`](case-461-reader-before-background-kill.png).
2. Pressed Home, opened Samsung Recents, and confirmed the Parrot task card remained (`taskView desc="Parrot"`). `adb shell kill -9 2412` was rejected by the device shell (`Operation not permitted`), so used the Android-supported background process command `adb -s RFCWC0SSVDM shell am kill com.retro99.parrot`. `pidof` then returned no PID while Recents remained the resumed activity and Parrot's task card remained visible.
3. Tapped the existing Parrot card in Recents. New process PID `8228` opened the Reader route; toolbar/title and 49% indicator appeared, but the reading-content surface was solid black. Screenshots: [immediate result](case-461-after-retained-task-kill.png), [after tapping the Reader surface](case-461-after-retained-task-tap.png), and [settled observation](case-461-after-retained-task-settled.png).
4. The content remained black for at least 30 seconds. Tapping the surface restored the toolbar but not publication text. The local process stayed alive; no `FATAL EXCEPTION` or `Fatal signal` was found in the inspected PID/system log window.
