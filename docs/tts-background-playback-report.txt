Known problems I am leaving:
 - Recorded narration does not start playing on this phone (pre-existing).
 - A one-page section does not auto-advance: read-aloud reads it to the end and stops
   with no next-chapter attempt. ReaderSyncCoordinator.onChapterCompleted waits for a
   locator whose href differs from the completed one, which such a section seems not to
   produce. Shared with the recorded-narration path.
 - Because the service is now kept through a chapter handover, a chapter end that does
   not auto-advance leaves the notification up showing a finished chapter until the app
   is idle-stopped or the user presses Stop listening. This is the price of the fix.
 - At every handover the playlist swap takes the player through IDLE, so Media3 calls
   startForegroundService on a service that is already foreground and the system logs
   "not allowed due to mAllowStartForeground false". Playback is unaffected and the
   service stays foreground, but the log line is alarming and worth tidying.
