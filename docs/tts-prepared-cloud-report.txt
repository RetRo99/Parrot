E: wrong behaviours found (list); fixed with test first (list); left and why (list):
Found, both in this feature's own code:
W1 the estimate shows the previous chapter's sentence count until read-aloud loads the new
   one. Opening a 9-sentence chapter straight after a 391-sentence one showed "About 15
   minutes · about 5 MB"; after pressing Play it corrected to "Under a minute · about 130
   kB". Cause: viewState.ttsSentenceCount is a controller-level value with no chapter
   attached, and ReaderOverlay feeds it to preparedChapterEstimate unconditionally.
W2 the prepared-chapter row's cloud line does not refresh when a transfer finishes. It
   stayed "Waiting to back up" after the upload was available and finalized on the server,
   and "Downloading from Parrot Cloud…" after the download was fully installed; both
   corrected only after an app restart. Cause: AndroidTtsController's preparedChapterAudio
   and preparedChapterCloud flows re-read only on chapterPreparationJob.state or
   preparedAudioRevision, and nothing bumps that revision when the transfer engine
   settles. The seam for the fix is PreparedChapterBackupQueue.watchOutcome, which already
   observes the transfer to a terminal state but is gated on analytics being non-null and
   signals nobody; the same is needed in PreparedChapterDownloadQueue, surfaced through
   TtsPreparedChapterBackup to refreshPreparedAudio().
