Places the prompt was ambiguous and what I chose:
Step 1: the notification text function also answers for "nothing running at all" (0 of 0
sentences). I kept the bare count there and show the promise only while a run exists.
Step 2: the brief said to keep the device's own measured figure where one exists, but there
are two - a flat bytes-a-sentence from past manifests and a measured audio length per
character. I prefer the audio length, because it knows this chapter's text; the flat figure
is kept for a device that has one but no audio length. The brief also did not ask for the
fixed spoken length to change, but its acceptance test cannot pass without it, so I raised
it and left the preparation-time figures alone.
Step 3: the brief named the four upload ends "uploaded, failed, storage full, not allowed".
The first three are the engine's terminal rows; "not allowed" is returned synchronously
before anything is queued, so I signal on that and on the other synchronous ends (refused,
pack rejected) as well, and not on the states that mean "later" or "nothing to do".
Step 3: the brief described a settable callback surfaced from TtsPreparedChapterBackup. I
used a StateFlow instead, because the facade is app-wide while a controller is per reader
screen, so a single settable callback would be overwritten by the last screen to attach;
and because a state flow is what answers a fresh collector at once.
